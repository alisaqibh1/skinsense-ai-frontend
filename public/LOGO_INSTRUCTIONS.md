# Logo and Favicon Setup Instructions

## Required Files:

### 1. Logo Image
**Location:** `frontend/src/assets/logo.png`
- **Size:** 512x512 pixels (or similar square dimensions)
- **Format:** PNG with transparent background
- **Design:** SkinSense AI logo (medical/skin analysis theme)
- **Colors:** Should work well on teal background (#0891B2)

### 2. Favicon
**Location:** `frontend/public/favicon.png`
- **Size:** 32x32 pixels or 64x64 pixels
- **Format:** PNG
- **Design:** Simplified version of the logo or icon

## How to Add:

1. **Create the assets folder:**
   ```
   frontend/src/assets/
   ```

2. **Place your logo:**
   - Save your logo as `logo.png` in `frontend/src/assets/`

3. **Place your favicon:**
   - Save your favicon as `favicon.png` in `frontend/public/`

## Temporary Solution:

If you don't have a logo yet, you can:
1. Use a placeholder image
2. Or revert to using the Scan icon from lucide-react

## Design Suggestions:

- Use medical/healthcare symbols (stethoscope, shield, scan icon)
- Incorporate skin/dermatology elements
- Keep it simple and recognizable
- Use colors from the palette: #DC2626 (red), #0891B2 (teal), #DCFCE7 (light green)
